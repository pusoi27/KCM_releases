## Editing Notes

- Recommended style: clean UI zooms + light parallax on stills.
- Transition: 250–350ms cross-dissolve.
- Background music: upbeat, professional, low-intensity.
- Keep text overlays to <= 7 words each.
- Ideal output: 1080p, 30fps, H.264.
